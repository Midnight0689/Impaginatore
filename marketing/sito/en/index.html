Features

From the first spread to the last, the same album.

The app does the long, repetitive parts. The choices stay yours.

Pro and Studio

A draft of the whole album

Impaginatore groups your photos in shooting order and picks, for each group, a layout that respects the proportions of every shot. You start from a whole album to review, not from a blank page.

  • Panoramas on their own, across the spread
  • Portrait and landscape where they sit best
  • Every spread stays editable by hand
Pro and Studio

Equal spacing and tone across the album

The gap between photos and the margins stay the same from one spread to the next. The check lists the spreads that don’t match, with the reference measure, and fixes them all together. In the same way you can even out brightness and colour between photos of the same scene.

  • Spacing check on every spread
  • Fix the whole album, or one spread at a time
  • Consistent tone between neighbouring photos
All editions

Refine spread by spread

Rulers in centimetres, guides, snapping and layouts that adapt to the photos you pick. Move, swap, crop: you decide the final spread, and the app keeps track of the measurements.

  • Designer layouts and your own
  • A warning when a photo is cropped too much
  • Saved versions, to go back
Pro and Studio

Layered PSD for Photoshop

Any spread can come out as a Photoshop file with every photo on its own layer, already at print size. Retouch only where needed, without redoing the layout. PSD export needs Photoshop installed, on Mac or Windows; JPG and PDF are made by Impaginatore on its own.

All editions

Your lab’s formats

When you create the album you choose lab, size and cover: bleed, gutter and safety margins are already right. If your lab isn’t listed, you set size and bleed once and save them.

Your photos stay on your computer

Impaginatore works on the files on your disk. There is no account, no service to upload them to, and the app doesn’t send them anywhere.

Pay once

The licence is in your name and doesn’t expire. It includes every update of the version you buy; the next major version is optional.

Works offline

The licence is checked on your computer, without contacting a server. Lay out and export while travelling or in a studio with no network.

Pro and Studio · optional

If you use Claude, it can draft the album with you.

Pick the photos and tell Claude how you want the album; it works inside Impaginatore while you watch, and every spread stays yours to change. You can also never use it: the app works the same way.

Needs your own Claude subscription, paid separately. Claude receives album data (file names, proportions, shooting time), not the photos, unless you allow it.

How it works

Three steps, from your disk to the lab.

  1. Create the album

    Choose size and lab, then the folder with your selected photos. Impaginatore reads them from the disk and puts them in shooting order.

  2. Start from the draft

    With Pro and Studio you get a draft of the whole album. With Essential you build one spread at a time, with layouts suggested for the photos you pick.

  3. Refine and export

    Adjust the spreads, check spacing and tone across the album, then export JPG for the lab, a PDF for the couple or PSD for Photoshop.

Why it exists

Born from an app that will stop opening.

Impaginatore comes from the workbench of someone who has been laying out albums for more than ten years, and still does it with an app bought in 2012. When Macs stop running apps written for the old Intel processors, that app won’t open any more, and the alternative was a subscription. So he built his own, and sells it the way he would have liked to buy it: once.

Questions

Frequently asked questions.

Are my photos uploaded anywhere?

No. Impaginatore reads your photos from the folder you choose, shows them and exports the files to your disk. There is no account and no online service to upload them to.

The app isn’t signed by Apple or Microsoft: can I trust it?

The installers don’t carry an Apple or Microsoft signature. Every file is signed with our own key and has a published SHA-256 fingerprint: the install command checks them by itself and stops if anything doesn’t match. With the classic installer, the system asks you to confirm the first time: on Mac in System Settings, Privacy & Security, Open Anyway; on Windows with More info, Run anyway. How to check a file by hand.

Can I get a refund?

Yes. Within 30 days of purchase we refund the full amount, without asking why. It applies once per customer and per edition. Before buying you can also try it free for 30 days.

How many computers can I use it on?

Essential on 1 computer, Pro on 2, Studio on 5. The licence is in your name and the app checks it on your computer, without contacting a server: the number of computers is a contractual commitment, not a lock. If you change computer, install on the new one and stop using the old one.

Are updates included?

Yes, every update of the version you buy: fixes and new features. A new major version comes out no sooner than 18 months after the previous one; moving to it is optional, costs half the list price of your edition and is included with Studio. If you bought in the 90 days before it comes out, you get it free. If you don’t move, your version keeps working.

Do I need an internet connection?

Only to download the app and, if you want, the updates. The licence is checked on your computer: you can lay out and export offline.

What happens when the trial ends?

The trial lasts 30 days, has the Pro features except PSD and puts a watermark on exported files. Afterwards your projects still open in read-only mode: enter the key and carry on where you left off.

Do I need Photoshop?

No. Impaginatore makes JPG and PDF files itself. Photoshop is only needed for layered PSD: Impaginatore prepares them with the Photoshop installed on your computer, on Mac or Windows.

Is the app in English?

The interface is in Italian today. The English version is planned before the launch; this page will say so when it’s ready.

How do I buy?

Choose the edition and send us your invoice details; we reply with the amount and you pay by bank transfer. The key arrives by email within 2 working hours of payment. If you’re using the trial, enter the key and carry on with the same album.

Which systems does it run on?

macOS (Apple Silicon and Intel), Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit Linux. Minimum memory and disk: to be set after testing on all three systems.

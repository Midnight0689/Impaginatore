Features

From the first spread to the last, the same album.

The app does the long, repetitive parts. The choices stay yours.

Pro and Studio

Draft the whole album

«Draft the album» lays out every photo you marked to use, in shooting order. It starts a new chapter when more than half an hour passes between two shots and gives a page of its own to panoramas and, if you like, to your favourites. If you don’t like the proposal, ask for another. It needs at least 10 photos. You start from a whole album to review, not from a blank page.

  • Rhythm: full bleed, white borders or alternating
  • Photos per spread: you choose, or the app does
  • One undo step; every spread stays editable
All editions

Check, then Fix

«Check» looks for what prints badly: missing or damaged photos, resolution too low for the frame, photos running off the page, heavy crops, faces cut or close to the gutter, uneven gaps between photos, empty pages, photos used twice, photos lighter or darker than the rest of the album. It tells you whether the album is ready to print and, with a click, takes you to the spread. «Fix» corrects spacing and tone by itself, in one step you can undo.

  • The same check in every edition
  • With Pro and Studio «Fix» works on the whole album, tone included
  • With Essential it fixes the open spread
All editions

Next layout, then refine

Press D and the same photos move into another layout; D again for the next, Esc to go back to how it was. Then refine by hand: rulers in centimetres, guides, snapping, S to replace a photo, text with your own styles. You decide the final spread, and the app keeps track of the measurements.

  • Ready layouts for the photos you pick, and your own templates
  • Resolution in ppi for every photo, and a crop warning
  • Automatic saving and saved versions
Pro and Studio

Layered PSD for Photoshop

Any spread can come out as a Photoshop file with every photo and every text on its own layer. Retouch only where needed, without redoing the layout. PSD files are made by the Photoshop installed on your computer, on Mac or Windows; on Linux they can’t be made. JPG and PDF are made by Impaginatore on its own.

All editions

Formats and lab catalogues

You create the album with a ready size or with the measurements your lab gives you, bleed included. If the lab has a catalogue for Impaginatore, you import it and pick the product: size, bleed and resolution come in already right, the check follows its rules and «Lab delivery» exports the JPG files with the names and sizes it asks for, with a summary to send along.

The app includes a catalogue of common sizes that belongs to no lab. There are no catalogues from real labs yet.

Your photos stay on your computer

Impaginatore works on the files on your disk and doesn’t change them. There is no account, no service to upload them to, and the app doesn’t send them anywhere. The one exception is yours to make: reduced images for the AI assistant, off by default.

Pay once

The licence is in your name and doesn’t expire. It includes every update of the version you buy; the next major version is optional.

Works offline

The licence is checked on your computer, without contacting a server. Lay out and export while travelling or in a studio with no network.

In English and Italian

Choose the app’s language from the Albums screen; the first time, it uses your computer’s language.

It saves by itself

The app saves while you work and keeps earlier versions, so you can go back hours, not just a few steps.

The album goes where you go

A copy of the album, with or without the photos, in a folder or a ZIP file, to open on another computer.

Pro and Studio · optional

If you use Claude, it can work on the album with you.

Connect Claude Desktop or Claude Code to Impaginatore and ask in plain words: «draft the album with three photos per spread», «check the album», «try another layout on spread 4». Claude works in the app while you watch, and every change it makes is undone with Ctrl+Z, like yours. It can’t delete albums or photos, or export PSD. You can also never use it: the app works the same way.

Needs Claude Desktop or Claude Code with your own Claude account, paid separately. Claude receives text: names of albums, folders and files, sizes, dates, the text written in the album, the check results; all of it goes to Anthropic, under its own terms. Images are off by default: photo thumbnails and spread previews are sent only if you turn them on, the originals never.

How it works

Three steps, from your disk to the lab.

  1. Create the album

    Choose the folder with your selected photos and the size: a ready one, your lab’s measurements or a product from its catalogue. Impaginatore reads the photos from the disk, without copying them.

  2. Start from the draft

    With Pro and Studio «Draft the album» lays out the whole album, in shooting order. With Essential you build one spread at a time, with layouts suggested for the photos you pick.

  3. Check and export

    Change layouts with D, «Check» tells you what prints badly and «Fix» corrects spacing and tone. Then export JPG for the lab, a PDF for the couple or PSD for Photoshop.

Why it exists

Born from an app that will stop opening.

Impaginatore comes from the workbench of someone who has been laying out albums for more than ten years, and still does it with an app bought in 2012. When Macs stop running apps written for the old Intel processors, that app won’t open any more, and the alternative was a subscription. So he built his own, and sells it the way he would have liked to buy it: once.

Questions

Frequently asked questions.

Are my photos uploaded anywhere?

No. Impaginatore reads your photos from the folder you choose, shows them and exports the files to your disk. There is no account and no online service to upload them to. If you connect Claude, it receives reduced images only if you turn them on; the originals never.

How do I install it?

With the installer for your system: Mac with Apple chip or Intel, Windows 10 and 11, Linux (Ubuntu 24.04 and derivatives). You can’t download it yet: for now the files go to people taking part in the private beta. The files are not signed with Apple or Microsoft certificates, so the first time your system shows a warning; the installation guide explains how to open the program and how to check the file.

Can I get a refund?

Yes. Within 30 days of purchase we refund the full amount, without asking why. It applies once per customer and per edition. Before buying you can also try it free for 30 days.

How many computers can I use it on?

Essential on 1 computer, Pro on 2, Studio on 5. The licence is in your name and the app checks it on your computer, without contacting a server: the number of computers is a contractual commitment, not a lock. If you change computer, remove the licence from the old one («Remove from this computer») and enter it on the new one.

Are updates included?

Yes, every update of the version you buy: fixes and new features. A new major version comes out no sooner than 18 months after the previous one; moving to it is optional, costs half the list price of your edition and is included with Studio. If you bought in the 90 days before it comes out, you get it free. If you don’t move, your version keeps working.

Do I need an internet connection?

No. The licence is checked on your computer: you can lay out and export offline. Internet is only needed by the AI assistant, if you use it.

What happens when the trial ends?

The trial lasts 30 days, has the Pro features except PSD and puts a watermark on exported files. Afterwards your projects still open in read-only mode: enter the key and carry on where you left off.

Do I need Photoshop?

No. Impaginatore makes JPG and PDF files itself. Photoshop is only needed for layered PSD: Impaginatore prepares them with the Photoshop installed on your computer, on Mac or Windows.

Is the app in English?

Yes. The app is in English and Italian: change it from «⋯», «Language…» on the Albums screen. The first time, it uses your computer’s language. A few labels of the built-in sizes catalogue are still in Italian.

How do I buy?

Choose the edition and send us your invoice details; we reply with the amount and you pay by bank transfer. The key arrives by email within 2 working hours of payment. If you’re using the trial, paste the key into the «Licence» window on the Albums screen and carry on with the same album.

Which systems does it run on?

It is made for macOS, Windows and Linux. The Linux package (Ubuntu 24.04 and derivatives) has been tested; the macOS and Windows ones are being tested, and the tests on real Mac and Windows computers will set the minimum memory and disk requirements. On Linux you can’t make layered PSDs.

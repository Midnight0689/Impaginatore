IMPAGINATORE IS READY. YOU PAY ONCE.

It’s an app for laying out wedding albums on your own computer, on Mac, Windows or Linux. You buy it once, with a licence that never expires: no subscription.

- Your photos stay on your computer. Impaginatore works on the files you already have. You upload nothing, and it works offline too.
- A check before printing. Pick a ready-made size or type the measurements, with bleed and margins. Before export, “Check” flags missing photos, photos under 150 ppi and photos outside the page.
- Layered PSD, if you retouch in Photoshop. With Pro and Studio every spread opens in Photoshop with each photo on its own layer. Photoshop is not included, and it is needed on macOS or Windows, not on Linux.
